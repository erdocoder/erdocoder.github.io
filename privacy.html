Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 9, 2026

ShaboTech operates the mobile application Equation Genius. Equation Genius is a math puzzle game with single-player and multiplayer modes, Apple Game Center integration, Apple CloudKit support for multiplayer, and usage analytics.

This Privacy Policy explains how Equation Genius handles information when you use the application. ShaboTech does not sell, rent, or share personal information for advertising or marketing purposes.

Information We Do Not Collect

Equation Genius does not collect:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or postal address.
  • Precise location information.
  • Your contacts, photos, or personal files.
  • Any game answers or equations you enter while playing.

The game supports both offline and online play. Personal information is never sold. Some information is processed to provide app features and to understand and improve how the app is used.

Data Used by the App

1. Game Center

If you sign in with Apple Game Center, Apple provides a unique player identifier that enables:

  • Leaderboards
  • Achievements (when enabled)
  • Multiplayer matchmaking

Apple manages all Game Center account information. ShaboTech only receives the identifier necessary for gameplay.

2. Multiplayer

When participating in multiplayer games, the app may temporarily use:

  • Apple Game Center player identifiers
  • Match room codes
  • Room status (host/guest and connection state)
  • Apple CloudKit services required to support multiplayer functionality

Multiplayer data is processed through Apple's Game Center and CloudKit services solely to establish and maintain active game sessions. ShaboTech does not use this information for advertising or marketing purposes.

3. Usage Analytics

Equation Genius uses Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the app is used and improve the gaming experience. This includes general usage information, gameplay statistics, and technical information about the app and device. Analytics data is not intentionally linked to your Apple Game Center identity and is not used by ShaboTech for advertising purposes.

Analytics may include:

  • App launches
  • Button selections
  • Match starts and completions
  • Feature usage frequency
  • Device model and operating system version

ShaboTech does not intentionally send your name, email address, Apple Game Center player identifier, game answers, or personal content to Firebase Analytics.

Firebase Analytics event data is retained for 2 months. User data is retained for 14 months, with the period reset when new activity is recorded. These settings do not affect aggregated reporting.

Firebase processes analytics data according to Google's Privacy Policy:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

Third-Party Services

Equation Genius uses the following third-party services:

  • Apple Game Center
  • Apple CloudKit
  • Google Firebase Analytics

These services are governed by their own privacy policies.

Legal Bases

Game Center and multiplayer information is processed as necessary to provide the features you request (performance of a contract). Firebase Analytics data is processed for our legitimate interest in understanding and improving the app. This GDPR basis does not remove any consent requirement that may apply under ePrivacy law.

International Data Processing

Apple and Google may process information in countries outside your country of residence. They describe the safeguards used for international transfers in their respective privacy and data-protection terms.

Apple Privacy Policy:
https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

Local Device Data

Equation Genius stores basic game data locally on your device, including:

  • Game settings and preferences.
  • Scores and gameplay progress.

Some multiplayer-related information may be processed through Apple's CloudKit services to support online gameplay. Apple manages this infrastructure according to its own privacy policies.

Cookies

The Equation Genius mobile application does not use cookies. The accompanying website may use standard browser cookies required for normal website functionality.

Security

We rely on Apple's secure services and Google's secure Firebase infrastructure. While we use industry-standard security measures, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure.

Children's Privacy

Equation Genius does not ask children to provide names, email addresses, or other contact details. Firebase Analytics may collect the usage and technical information described above. If you believe personal information has been submitted through external communication, please contact us.

Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights regarding data processed through the app. Questions or requests can be sent to ShaboTech at the email below. You may also contact your local data protection authority.

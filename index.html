Equation Genius

Solve. Improve. Compete.

Equation Genius is an equation game where you solve algebra-style math puzzles, compete for faster times, and progress through increasingly challenging equations. Every equation is automatically generated, and all solutions are whole integers.

Play Mode

Progress level by level, improve your speed and accuracy, and compete on the Game Center leaderboard.

Daily Challenge

Solve a new equation every day, build your streak, and climb the seasonal leaderboard with monthly resets.

Practice Mode

Master individual levels at your own pace and improve your personal bests.

Multiplayer Mode

Compete through Quick Match, challenge a friend with a room code, or practice against AI.

More ways to play

Share equations with friends using playable links and see how quickly they can solve them. Shared equations are quick, timed challenges.

Key features

  • Auto-generated algebra-style equations
  • Whole-number answers—no decimals or fractions
  • New equation types at higher levels
  • Time-based scoring that rewards speed
  • Daily streaks and seasonal rankings
  • Some advanced equations have multiple solutions

Available on iPhone and iPad · Compatible with Mac

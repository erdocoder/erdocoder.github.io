Equation Genius

Solve. Improve. Compete.

Equation Genius is a fast-paced equation game where you solve algebra puzzles, race against the clock, and progress through increasingly challenging mathematics. A wide variety of equation patterns keeps each run fresh, and all required answers are integers.

Solo Play

Progress through increasingly challenging levels, beat your personal best, and climb the Game Center leaderboard.

Compete

Race virtual rivals, win matches, earn Rank Score, and climb from Beginner to Genius.

Practice

Master individual levels, improve your level records, and prepare for more advanced equations.

Live Multiplayer

Challenge a friend using a room code, or try Quick Match when another player is available.

More ways to play

Take on a new Daily Challenge, build your streak, and join monthly leaderboard seasons. You can also share equations with friends using playable links.

Key features

  • Solo Play with increasingly challenging levels
  • Global Game Center leaderboard for Solo scores
  • Virtual rivals and Beginner-to-Genius rankings
  • Practice with personal level records
  • A wide variety of algebra-style equations
  • Gradual progression with new patterns and structures
  • Integer answers only—no decimals or fractions
  • Some advanced equations have multiple valid solutions
